I am currently raising money to help fund the tools and materials needed to build the actual device. The Raspberry Pi and other equipment needed to put everything together can add up quickly, so fundraising will help cover these costs and allow me to continue developing the machine. At the same time, I hope to use this opportunity to raise awareness about the project and its goal of making communication more accessible.
